Proprietary Framework

The PRISM Framework.

Five disciplined lenses for inflexion-point investing — phase identification, research intensity, inflexion signal recognition, scalability, and moat mapping.

P
Phase IdentificationMap each company's business phase — early-growth, scaling, dominant/mature, disrupted. Invest only in early-growth or scaling phases with a 5+ year runway; systematically disqualify mature and disrupted businesses.
R
Research-Intensive Due DiligenceMinimum 90-day primary research per position: management meets, customer interviews, supply-chain mapping, a 5-year financial model with scenario analysis, and regulatory/policy tailwind mapping.
I
Inflexion Signal RecognitionA proprietary 7-signal checklist: earnings acceleration, capacity addition, management upgrade, order-backlog surge, export-market entry, margin-expansion trend, or share capture from larger incumbents.
S
Scalability QuotientTotal Addressable Market must be ≥10x current revenues, with demonstrated unit economics that improve at scale. Prefer capital-light models with high operating leverage.
M
Moat MappingIdentify and document the specific moat — switching cost, network effect, cost advantage, intangible asset, or efficient scale. Re-validate annually; the exit thesis is constructed at inception, not at sell time.
Portfolio Construction

A Three-Tier Architecture.

Secular Growth Core — 40%

8–10 stocks. Five-year-plus compounders held for the full cycle — the durable engine of the portfolio.

Inflexion Opportunities — 35%

10–12 stocks where a specific inflexion signal has been identified. 2–3 year horizon to capture the non-linear acceleration.

Pre-IPO / Pre-Listing — 15%

3–4 positions sourced through the SPVH deal-flow network, with an 18–24 month liquidity-event horizon.

Tactical Cash — 10%

Dry powder deployed on corrections greater than 15% or when new inflexion signals emerge.

What Sets This Fund Apart.

A Unified Alpha Machine

Timing, Not Just Quality

Phase identification adds a dimension most quality-growth funds miss — the timing of entry, capturing the moment of maximum change rather than paying up after the fact.

Agile by Design

A focused fund size preserves agility in mid-and-small-cap inflexion names that larger, capacity-heavy funds cannot meaningfully access.
