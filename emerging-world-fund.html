Proprietary Framework

The WORLD Investment Framework.

Five disciplined pillars combining top-down macro country rotation, a cost-efficient FoF + direct hybrid, risk-calibrated country bands, on-ground local intelligence, and dollar-return optimisation — capturing the EM growth premium that passive ETF feeders miss entirely.

W
Winner Country Selection (Top-Down Macro Rotation)Proprietary 5-factor country scoring matrix — GDP growth momentum, earnings revision cycle, valuation vs 5-year mean, currency stability, geopolitical risk score. Updated quarterly. Determines country allocation bands (min 5%, max 35% per country). Prevents static allocation drag — the #1 failure of passive EM ETFs.
O
Optimised FoF + Direct Hybrid ArchitectureFor markets where SPVH lacks deep local research (e.g. Vietnam), deploy via institutional share class of proven local-expert EM managers (Robeco, Matthews Asia, KAM Korea). For high-conviction, clearly-valued markets (China Internet, Korea Semis, Indonesia banking), invest directly in listed equities — reducing fee layers while maintaining research quality.
R
Risk-Calibrated Country BandsEach country allocation has a predefined risk budget. High-conviction, lower-risk countries (Korea, Taiwan): up to 30%. High-growth, higher-risk countries (Vietnam, Indonesia): capped at 15% each. Prevents tail-risk concentration while ensuring the portfolio actually captures EM outperformance — unlike S&P 500 feeders that miss EM entirely.
L
Local Intelligence NetworkSPVH's existing Asia and ASEAN JV partnerships (12+ JV partners across SPVH Group) provide on-ground intelligence in key EM markets — the same network used for SPVH's I-Banking consulting in ASEAN, now monetised into investment alpha. Local management meets, supply-chain analysis, and regulatory monitoring in each target market.
D
Dollar-Return OptimisationEvery investment is analysed in USD terms — not local currency. Currency hedge overlay considered for high-depreciation-risk countries (e.g. Indonesian Rupiah). Target is 20–28% USD net return — not local currency returns that can be eroded by 10–15% depreciation. INR/USD tailwind also benefits Indian LPs investing via LRS.

Conviction Themes

High-Conviction Sector Themes by Country.

Greater China — AI & Consumer Recovery

China Internet (Tencent, Alibaba), DeepSeek-driven AI hardware demand, semiconductor EDA tools, and domestic consumer-discretionary recovery. Hong Kong-listed H-shares at 60–70% discount to intrinsic value. Taiwan TSMC — an irreplaceable AI chip supply-chain node.

South Korea — Governance Reform + AI Semis

Samsung Electronics at a historic valuation discount. SK Hynix HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) — critical to every AI datacenter globally. Korea's governance reform (Value-Up programme) creating a re-rating opportunity. Korean financials (KB Financial) benefiting from reform-led multiple expansion.

Vietnam — Manufacturing Ascent

China+1 supply-chain relocation. Vietnam is the #1 beneficiary of global manufacturing diversification. Samsung, Apple, and LG already operate major hubs. Local textile, electronics, and logistics plays at an early growth stage. GDP 6%+ growth driven by export manufacturing.

Indonesia — Domestic Consumption Giant

280 million population. Banking penetration <40%. Digital-payment adoption accelerating. GoTo, Bank Central Asia, and Mandiri at early-stage middle-class expansion. Indonesia represents the EM opportunity India was 15 years ago — massive domestic demand, underbanked, urbanising rapidly.

Hybrid Architecture

FoF vs Direct Equity.

FoF Layer — 40–50% of Portfolio

Institutional share-class allocations to proven EM managers: Robeco Emerging Stars (Korea/Taiwan overweight), Matthews Asia Emerging Markets (China + SEA), JPMorgan Greater China. Negotiated institutional fees (0.3–0.6% vs retail 1.5%). Managers selected using a 5-factor quantitative screen: IR, Sharpe, downside capture, manager tenure, AUM stability.

Direct Equity Layer — 45–55% of Portfolio

High-conviction direct positions where SPVH has local research capability and strong conviction: Korea semiconductors (Samsung, SK Hynix), China internet (Tencent, Alibaba H-shares), Indonesia banking (BCA, Bank Mandiri), and Vietnam ETFs as proxy. The direct layer reduces total fee drag by 80–120 bps vs pure FoF.
