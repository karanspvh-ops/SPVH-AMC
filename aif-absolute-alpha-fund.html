Proprietary Framework

The EDGE Framework.

Four pillars for true long-short alpha — an evidence-driven long book, a disruption-aware short book, dynamic exposure management, and event-driven pair trades.

E
Evidence-Driven Long Book20–25 long positions in structurally advantaged, quality businesses — each backed by 90-day diligence. The long book is the primary alpha engine (PRISM-compatible). No momentum chasing; only evidence-based conviction.
D
Disruption-Aware Short Book8–12 short positions on structurally impaired businesses — losing share to new entrants, overleveraged at earnings peak, or cyclicals topping out. Individual-stock shorts via F&O — no index shorts.
G
Gross & Net Exposure ManagementDynamic net exposure (40–80%). Bull markets: net-long skew 70–80%. Bear/volatile: reduce to 40–50% net. Max gross 160%. Leverage only via derivatives, never borrowed equity.
E
Event-Driven Pair TradesSector pair trades — long the high-quality player, short the structurally weak peer. Neutralises sector risk and extracts company-quality alpha. 4–6 active pair trades at any time.
Strategy in Action

Long & Short Thesis by Market Condition.

Bull Market Strategy

Net-long 70–80%. Long book fully deployed; short book reduced to structural/secular shorts only. Pair trades remain active to capture the quality spread.

Bear / Volatile Market Strategy

Net-long reduced to 40–50%. Short book expanded to 12+ positions including cyclical and event-driven shorts. Target: positive absolute return when the Nifty drops 10%+.

Long Book Themes (2025–27)

Defence/aerospace electronics, capital-goods EPC, private-sector capex, asset-light financial intermediaries, and India-facing technology platform businesses.

Short Book Themes (2025–27)

Legacy media/print, commoditised IT services without AI differentiation, overleveraged real-estate developers, legacy PSUs at earnings peak, and durables losing to D2C disruption.
