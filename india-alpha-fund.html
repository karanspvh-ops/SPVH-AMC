Portfolio Construction

A Three-Tier Architecture.

Core India Equity — 70%

18–22 stocks across India's structural growth themes: capital goods, defence, domestic financial services, technology-enabled businesses, and speciality manufacturing. 3–5 year hold. ROCE ≥ 18%, debt-light, with a management quality filter.

Pre-IPO / Event Bridge — 15%

3–5 positions. Pre-listing India opportunities from the SPVH AMC pipeline and anchor book allocations. 12–24 month liquidity-event horizon. Proprietary sourcing through the SPVH Value Discovery Fund deal-flow.

EM Asia Optionality — 10–15%

High-conviction positions where India equity lacks equivalent exposure: Taiwan/Korea AI semiconductor supply chain, ASEAN digital-economy leaders, and China internet/consumer recovery plays. Adds EM alpha unavailable in India-only mandates.

Cash / FX Buffer — 0–10%

USD-denominated liquid instruments during high-valuation / low-opportunity periods. Actively managed — not idle. Deployed on 10%+ corrections in core India markets or the emergence of new pre-IPO opportunities.

The India Alpha Edge

What Sets This Fund Apart.

Four structural advantages that make India Alpha a true multi-alpha vehicle — combining active conviction, proprietary sourcing, institutional liquidity discipline, and a built-in currency tailwind in a single USD-denominated structure.

Multi-Alpha in One Vehicle

The ALPHA framework blends a high-conviction India core with EM Asia optionality and a pre-IPO event bridge — three distinct return engines combined in a single USD-denominated structure, not a one-dimensional India bet.

Proprietary Sourcing Moat

SPVH's UHNI network and the SPVH Value Discovery Fund deal pipeline open access to pre-IPO allocations and anchor books — a sourcing advantage that passive feeders and standalone managers simply cannot replicate.

Institutional Liquidity Discipline

Position sizes are capped at 4× average daily turnover so the portfolio can be fully liquidated in ≤ 15 trading days — a structured liquidity protocol built for institutional LP redemption management from day one.

Built-In Currency Tailwind

USD denomination means every basis point of INR depreciation (~3–4% p.a. historically) accrues as additional return to the global LP versus a domestic INR fund — active currency and redemption management included.
