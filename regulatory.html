Regulatory Framework

SPVH AMC operates within the regulatory framework established by the following authorities and legislation:

  • Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) — Primary regulator for securities markets, portfolio managers, AIFs, research analysts, and investment advisers.
  • International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA) — Regulator for financial products and services within GIFT City IFSC.
  • Companies Act, 2013 — Governing corporate structure, compliance, and governance requirements.
  • Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 (PMLA) — AML/KYC compliance obligations.
  • Income Tax Act, 1961 — Tax compliance and reporting obligations for investors and the company.
  • Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 (FEMA) — Regulations governing cross-border investments and offshore fund operations.

Governance & Compliance

SPVH AMC is committed to maintaining the highest standards of corporate governance and regulatory compliance:

  • Board Oversight: The Board of Directors provides strategic direction and oversight of all business activities, ensuring alignment with regulatory requirements and fiduciary obligations.
  • Compliance Function: A dedicated compliance team monitors adherence to all applicable regulations, internal policies, and industry best practices.
  • Risk Management: Comprehensive risk management frameworks are in place covering market risk, operational risk, credit risk, and compliance risk.
  • KYC & AML: Robust Know Your Client and Anti-Money Laundering procedures are implemented in accordance with SEBI, RBI, and PMLA requirements.
  • Conflict of Interest: Policies and procedures are established to identify, manage, and disclose potential conflicts of interest.

Investor Obligations

Investors engaging with SPVH AMC's products and services are advised to:

  • Read all scheme-related documents, including the Private Placement Memorandum (PPM), Contribution Agreement, and Disclosure Document, carefully before investing.
  • Complete all KYC formalities and provide accurate information as required under applicable regulations.
  • Understand the risks associated with investments in securities markets, including the possibility of loss of capital.
  • Seek independent professional advice from qualified financial advisers, tax consultants, and legal counsel.
  • Report any grievances or complaints through the designated channels provided by SPVH AMC.
