Proprietary Framework

The IMPACT Framework.

Six disciplined pillars that fuse structural India conviction with management quality, earnings rigour, valuation discipline, and a rule-based cycle protocol.

I
India-First Thematic Lens70% core allocation to structural New India themes: manufacturing, defence, EMS, capital goods, domestic financial services — megatrends, not sectors.
M
Management Quality ScoreProprietary 8-point governance and capital-allocation scorecard. Filters for exceptional promoter-manager alignment and track record.
P
Profit Trajectory AnalysisInvest only where EPS can double within 3 years. Earnings momentum validated by a sector-level model, not consensus estimates.
A
Adequate Margin of SafetySatellite 30% allocation: opportunistic / contrarian picks. Entry only when forward P/E is ≤60% of addressable growth optionality.
C
Cycle Awareness ProtocolPredefined macro signals trigger defensive repositioning: reduce to 12–14 stocks, raise cash to 20–25%, hedge via index puts. Rule-based, not discretionary.
T
Thesis Review CadenceQuarterly thesis review for all holdings. Exit discipline: if the original thesis breaks — not the price — we exit regardless of MTM.

The Ascent Edge

What Sets This Strategy Apart.

Theme + Cycle in One Engine

The IMPACT framework fuses macro-cycle triggers with structural New India theme conviction — a reproducible, auditable alpha engine rather than a single-factor bet.

Rule-Based Downside Protection

A predefined cycle-awareness protocol raises cash and adds hedges at macro stress signals — protecting capital without relying on discretionary timing.

Concentrated Yet Diversified

A 20–25 stock portfolio is concentrated enough for conviction but diversified enough to materially reduce single-stock blow-up risk.

Scalable Without Diluting Edge

Multi-cap flexibility preserves upside while remaining scalable to ₹3,000 Cr — capacity that capacity-constrained small-cap-only peers cannot match.
